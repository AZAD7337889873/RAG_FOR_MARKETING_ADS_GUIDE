Meta Ads Platform Guidelines

Meta Ads can support:

- Awareness
- Traffic
- Engagement
- Lead generation
- Conversions
- Ecommerce sales

Creative performance can vary significantly across audiences and
placements.

Important metrics include:

- Impressions
- Reach
- Frequency
- CTR
- CPC
- Conversion Rate
- Conversions
- Revenue
- ROAS

High impressions do not necessarily indicate good business performance.

A campaign should be evaluated across the funnel.

If CTR is strong but conversion rate is weak, investigate:

- Landing page
- Offer
- Audience quality
- Conversion tracking
- User experience

Creative-level performance should be reviewed when enough data exists.
