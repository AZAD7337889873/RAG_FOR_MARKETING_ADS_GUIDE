LinkedIn Ads Platform Guidelines

LinkedIn Ads can be relevant for:

- B2B marketing
- Professional audiences
- Lead generation
- Account-based marketing
- Enterprise campaigns

Important metrics include:

- Impressions
- Clicks
- CTR
- CPC
- Leads
- Conversion Rate
- Cost per Lead
- Revenue

Cost per lead should not be evaluated independently from lead quality.

When downstream revenue data exists, campaign performance should also
be evaluated using revenue and return metrics.

LinkedIn campaigns can operate against smaller professional audiences,
which may affect delivery volume and advertising costs.
