Google Ads Platform Guidelines

Google Ads can support multiple marketing objectives including:

- Awareness
- Website traffic
- Lead generation
- Conversions
- Ecommerce sales

Search campaigns are particularly useful when users demonstrate active
search intent.

Shopping campaigns can be useful for ecommerce products and product
discovery.

Important performance metrics include:

- Impressions
- Clicks
- CTR
- CPC
- Conversions
- Conversion Rate
- Revenue
- ROAS

CTR should not be evaluated independently when the business objective
is revenue or conversions.

A campaign with a high CTR but low conversion rate may require review
of the landing page, audience, offer or message.

Geographic, audience and device segments should be evaluated when enough
historical data exists.

Budget decisions should consider both efficiency and scalability.
