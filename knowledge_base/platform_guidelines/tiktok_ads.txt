TikTok Ads Platform Guidelines

TikTok Ads are primarily associated with short-form video advertising.

Important considerations include:

- Creative quality
- Video engagement
- Audience relevance
- CTR
- CPC
- Conversion Rate
- Conversion volume
- Revenue

Low CPM should not automatically be interpreted as better business
performance.

Creative-level analysis can be important because performance may vary
significantly between videos.

When sufficient data exists, compare:

- Creative
- Audience
- Placement
- Campaign
- Geography

Optimization should ultimately consider the business objective rather
than engagement metrics alone.
