Amazon Ads Platform Guidelines

Amazon Ads are highly relevant to ecommerce and product discovery.

Important metrics include:

- Impressions
- Clicks
- CTR
- CPC
- Orders
- Conversion Rate
- Sales
- Advertising Spend
- ROAS

Product-level and search-term-level analysis can identify differences
in performance.

ROAS should be considered together with:

- Product margin
- Incremental sales
- Scale
- Inventory
- Customer value

A high ROAS does not automatically mean a campaign should receive
additional budget.
