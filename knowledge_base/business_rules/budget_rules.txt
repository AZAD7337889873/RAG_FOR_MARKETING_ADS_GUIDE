Marketing Budget Rules

Rule 1

A single platform should not receive more than 40% of the total budget
through an automated recommendation.

Rule 2

A single recommendation should normally not change an existing campaign
budget by more than 20%.

Rule 3

Campaigns should not be reduced below the configured minimum daily
budget.

Rule 4

If spend exceeds planned budget, mark the campaign as:

OVER_PACING

Rule 5

If spend is materially below the expected plan, mark the campaign as:

UNDER_PACING

Rule 6

Budget recommendations must contain:

- Current budget
- Recommended budget
- Change amount
- Change percentage
- Reason
- Supporting evidence

Rule 7

Budget recommendations should consider historical performance.

Rule 8

A campaign should not receive additional budget solely because it has
a high CTR.

Rule 9

A campaign should not receive additional budget solely because it has
a high ROAS when its volume is extremely low.

Rule 10

If historical evidence is insufficient, return a data-insufficient
recommendation.
