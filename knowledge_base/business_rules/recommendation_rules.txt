Marketing Recommendation Rules

Rule 1

Do not recommend a platform solely because it has the lowest CPC.

Rule 2

Consider multiple performance metrics:

- CTR
- CPC
- Conversion Rate
- Conversion volume
- Revenue
- ROAS

Rule 3

Recommendations should use historical campaign evidence whenever
historical data exists.

Rule 4

Predictions generated by ML models must be described as estimates,
not guarantees.

Rule 5

Every recommendation should explain the evidence supporting it.

Rule 6

If insufficient historical data exists, explicitly state that the
available evidence is insufficient.

Rule 7

The LLM should not invent campaign metrics.

Rule 8

Structured campaign data and ML outputs have priority over generated
claims.

Rule 9

RAG content should provide marketing knowledge and business rules,
while actual historical metrics should come from the campaign dataset.

Rule 10

Recommendations should include assumptions when assumptions are
required.
