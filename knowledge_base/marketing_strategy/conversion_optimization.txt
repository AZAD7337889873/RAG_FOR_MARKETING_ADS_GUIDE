Conversion Optimization Framework

Conversion Rate is calculated as:

Conversion Rate = Conversions / Clicks

When conversion rate is low, investigate:

- Landing page experience
- Offer
- Audience quality
- Product-market relevance
- Conversion tracking
- Form or checkout friction

A campaign may have strong CTR but weak conversion rate.

This indicates that users are clicking but are not completing the
desired action at the expected rate.

Conversion optimization should consider:

- Conversion volume
- Conversion rate
- Cost per conversion
- Revenue
- ROAS

Recommendations should be based on sufficient historical data.
