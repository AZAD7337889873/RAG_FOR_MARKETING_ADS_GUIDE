Campaign Optimization Framework

Marketing performance should be analyzed as a funnel:

Impressions
    ->
Clicks
    ->
Conversions
    ->
Revenue

Common diagnostic patterns:

High impressions + low CTR

Potential areas to investigate:

- Creative relevance
- Audience targeting
- Messaging
- Placement

High CTR + low conversion rate

Potential areas to investigate:

- Landing page
- Offer
- Audience quality
- Conversion tracking
- User experience

Strong conversion rate + weak ROAS

Potential areas to investigate:

- CPC
- CPM
- Average order value
- Revenue per conversion
- Cost structure

Strong ROAS + low volume

Potential areas to investigate:

- Available audience
- Budget scalability
- Campaign reach
- Incremental performance

Historical campaign data should be used whenever possible.
