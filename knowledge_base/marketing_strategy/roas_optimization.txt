ROAS Optimization Framework

ROAS is calculated as:

ROAS = Revenue / Spend

ROAS can be used to understand revenue generated relative to advertising
spend.

Low ROAS can result from:

- High CPC
- High CPM
- Low conversion rate
- Low average order value
- Poor audience quality
- Tracking issues

A campaign with high ROAS may still have limited scalability.

Therefore ROAS should be evaluated together with:

- Spend
- Revenue
- Conversion volume
- Budget
- Scale
- Historical performance

ROAS should not be the only metric used for budget allocation.
