CTR Optimization Framework

CTR is calculated as:

CTR = Clicks / Impressions

Low CTR may indicate:

- Weak creative
- Poor audience relevance
- Weak messaging
- Incorrect placement
- Poor targeting

Potential optimization areas:

1. Test multiple creatives.
2. Test audience segments.
3. Review campaign messaging.
4. Review placement performance.
5. Compare current CTR with historical campaign CTR.

CTR should not be optimized independently when the business objective
is conversions or revenue.

A higher CTR does not necessarily produce higher revenue.
