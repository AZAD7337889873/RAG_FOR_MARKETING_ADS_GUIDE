Marketing Budget Allocation Framework

Budget allocation should begin with the campaign objective.

Possible objectives include:

- Awareness
- Traffic
- Lead generation
- Conversions
- Sales

Historical performance should be analyzed by:

- Platform
- Campaign
- Campaign type
- Geography
- Audience
- Channel

Important metrics include:

- Spend
- CPC
- CTR
- Conversion Rate
- CPA
- Revenue
- ROAS

Budget recommendations should consider both efficiency and scale.

A platform with strong ROAS but very low volume may not be able to
absorb a large budget increase.

A platform with lower efficiency but significant scale may still be
important to the overall marketing strategy.

Budget changes should normally be incremental.

A recommendation should include:

- Current budget
- Proposed budget
- Change percentage
- Supporting evidence
- Assumptions
- Expected impact
